CoRadio is a free, ad-free, open-source internet radio player. Listen to live radio stations from around the world on your phone, Android TV, Android Auto or Android Automotive.

<b>Features</b>
• Browse stations by country, genre, popularity or search
• Favorites and your own stations
• Import and export your stations
• Equalizer, sleep timer, stream buffering settings
• Chromecast and Bluetooth auto-play
• Material You dynamic colors on Android 12+

Station data comes from the Community Radio Browser and Web Radio DB directories. CoRadio contains no ads and no analytics.

CoRadio is a fork of OpenRadio by Yurii Chernyshov, licensed under Apache 2.0.
